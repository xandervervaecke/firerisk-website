Wildfire research is often concentrated on one moment: the fire itself. The *wildfire science loop* looks at the whole cycle (preventing fires, responding to them and recovering afterwards) and highlights that post-fire research has long been under-represented.

## Before: understanding danger, exposure and vulnerability

[WP1](/research/wp1-wui-mapping) maps where people and assets are exposed. [WP2](/research/wp2-microenvironment) and [WP3](/research/wp3-management-design) measure fuel, microclimate and the effect of land management, feeding the fire spread model of [WP4](/research/wp4-fire-spread-modelling).

## During: supporting the response

[WP5](/research/wp5-operational-decision-making) brings data and predictions into the command post, while [WP6](/research/wp6-ai-visualisation-toolkit) builds immersive, AI-driven simulations for training responders and raising citizens' awareness.

## After: resilience and recovery

[WP7](/research/wp7-resilience-recovery) compares how landscapes recover after uncontrolled wildfires and planned restoration fires, and develops decision support for fire-resilient management.

## Closing the loop

[WP8](/research/wp8-crisis-governance) connects all three stages in a crisis governance framework for Flanders, [WP9](/research/wp9-valorisation-dissemination) brings results into practice with stakeholders, and [WP10](/research/wp10-project-management) coordinates the project.
