FireRisk brings together five Flemish research institutions to strengthen wildfire risk assessment, prevention and response in Flanders. From today, this website is the central place to follow that work.

## What you will find here

- **[Research](/research)**: the ten work packages, organised along the wildfire science loop: before, during and after a fire.
- **[Team](/team)**: the researchers and staff behind the project. Profiles will be completed as the team takes shape.
- **[News](/news)**: project updates, field campaigns, publications and events.
- **[Contact](/contact)**: the consortium partners and how to get involved.

## Built for people who work with fire

FireRisk is designed together with the people who manage landscapes and respond to fires: land and nature managers, emergency planners and fire services, and policy-makers. If you work in one of these fields and would like to be involved, please [get in touch](/contact).
