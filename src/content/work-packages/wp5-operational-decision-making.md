## Goals

WP5 enhances decision-support systems and tools that integrate (near-)real-time multimodal remote sensing with the relevant outcomes of WP1–4, giving emergency services accurate operational insight. Predictive tools help fire services anticipate fire spread and allocate personnel and equipment more effectively.

The tools are embedded within existing crisis management structures, such as the command post of operations (CP-Ops), to support coordinated decision-making at local, regional and national levels. Scenario-based exercises help fire brigades, civil protection and crisis managers turn data-driven insight into swift, effective action.

## Tasks

### Task 5.1: Integrating dataflows into operational decision support

Results such as WUI presence (WP1), fuel characteristics (WP2) and fire spread predictions (WP4) are brought together in an integrated platform, researched for usability, interoperability and clear visualisation so that stakeholders can access critical information in real time.

### Task 5.2: Optimising resource allocation and response strategies

Based on simulations, a model is defined to support optimal response decisions by identifying the relevant variables from recent literature and stakeholder consultations, and assessing trade-offs such as between firefighting and evacuation.

### Task 5.3: Scenario-based simulations to refine emergency protocols

Emergency protocols are tested under varying and unexpected scenarios to identify gaps in decision-making and evaluate how resilient the protocols are under stress. Lessons learned feed continuous improvement.

### Task 5.4: Simulation-based training for firefighters

Realistic training environments let firefighters practise decision-making under pressure, coordination across roles and communication along the chain of command, in line with the governance networks identified in WP8.

### Task 5.5: Applicability and usability in a command post

Live tests in simulated settings, with role-play and scenario variation, evaluate both technical and human aspects, aligned with Task 8.5 at command post and coordination cell level.

### Task 5.6: Post-incident briefings

Established frameworks such as the Critical Decision Method and the Recognition-Primed Decision framework are used to analyse how professionals recognise cues, manage uncertainty and act under time pressure, feeding a continuous improvement loop.
