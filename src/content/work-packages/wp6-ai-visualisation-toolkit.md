## Goals

WP6 develops an interactive, AI-enabled visualisation tool to simulate realistic fire scenarios. Through immersive experiences it aims to strengthen risk perception, situational awareness and preparedness, delivering modular VR and CAVE training experiences for both citizens and emergency responders.

The work builds on data and models from WP1, WP2 and WP4 and is implemented and validated together with WP9.

## Tasks

### Task 6.1: High-fidelity 3D simulation environment

A highly realistic 3D environment is built from real-world data such as UAV imagery and LiDAR point clouds (WP2), able to display fire progression and changing conditions (weather, fuel loads, terrain) and to interact in real time with fire progression models (WP4). The task extends image-based reconstruction, simulation and parameter extraction of forest and urban regions towards large-scale simulations of wildland–urban interfaces.

### Task 6.2: AI-driven environment and scenario generation

Generative AI tools produce multiple simulation environments from the reconstructed "static" environment, including real-time, realistic simulations of fire. Scenarios are linked to the fire spread models of WP4 for dynamic response simulations.

### Task 6.3: Modular training modules

User-friendly, modular training modules for citizens and emergency responders include guided simulations, decision-making challenges and feedback mechanisms in VR and CAVE systems, fine-tuned towards the WUI.

### Task 6.4: Pilot testing and validation

The modules are tested in real-world settings such as community workshops and emergency service training drills (with WP5 and WP9). Feedback iteratively improves usability, realism and educational value.
