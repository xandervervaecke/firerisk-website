## Goals

WP10 ensures effective management and coordination of the project, so that the aims set out in the grant agreement are achieved.

## Tasks

### Task 10.1: Coordination bodies and tools

Roles and responsibilities are formalised in a consortium agreement, together with a clear communication strategy. A Project Advisory Board with one representative per end-user category ensures interaction with the end-user committee. A data management plan sets out how research data are handled, documented and shared.

### Task 10.2: Reporting and financial administration

Reporting follows the guidelines of the FWO. Each partner submits an internal progress report to the coordinator every six months. The scientific content is analysed by the work package leaders.

### Task 10.3: Quality control of deliverables

Major public deliverables follow a systematic review process: release by the WP leader, two weeks for partner feedback, two weeks for revisions and one week for final approval by the project board.

### Task 10.4: Internal and external communication

Consortium meetings every six months review progress at the case study sites, followed by a Project Board meeting. Two end-user committee meetings are organised each year. The project closes with two major dissemination events: a promotional campaign and a closing event.
