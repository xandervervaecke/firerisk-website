## Goals

WP9 prepares the valorisation and dissemination of FireRisk's key end products, so that research results are taken up by the people who manage landscapes and respond to fires.

## Tasks

### Task 9.1: Transfer to societal implementation

Activities that directly implement and facilitate the adoption of FireRisk's tools, data and frameworks, including discussing study sites and indicators with stakeholders, and validating, demonstrating and implementing research results.

### Task 9.2: Application-oriented activities

From the third project year onwards, research results are made more practical, user-friendly and aligned with the operational needs of end users, through pilot projects, regional implementation roadmaps, co-creation and feedback sessions, usability testing and user-centred development sprints.

### Task 9.3: Parallel R&D&I with societal actors

Exploring new research questions with societal actors and expanding the scope of applications that build on FireRisk.
