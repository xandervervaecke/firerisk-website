## Goals

Crisis governance is the design, governance and functioning of systems that anticipate and respond to crises in complex and dynamic environments. WP8 integrates all three stages of the wildfire science loop: it translates data generation (WP1–3), fire spread modelling (WP4) and training (WP5–6) into operational decision-making and strategic wildfire governance.

The emphasis lies on interorganisational coordination, on detecting and acting upon early warning signals, and on strengthening preparedness and response capacity in Flanders. Central elements are actors with shared mental models and decision-making capacity, resources and data that support collective action, and principles, protocols, processes and infrastructures for collaboration.

## Tasks

### Task 8.1: Case studies on interorganisational crisis governance

In-depth case studies identify enablers and barriers in wildfire governance networks, with a focus on the mental models needed for strategic coordination across organisational boundaries in Flanders.

### Task 8.2: Resource analysis for decision-making

Which resources (geodata, models, expertise, logistics) are critical for decision-making, and how can they be shared and mobilised across actors during a wildfire crisis?

### Task 8.3: Governance principles, protocols and processes

Existing frameworks for anticipation, collaboration and operational coordination before and during extreme fire events are assessed, with attention to integrating early warning indicators (WP2), spread models (WP4) and training modules (WP5–6).

### Task 8.4: Design of a crisis governance structure

A governance structure tailored to Flanders is designed to strengthen collaboration, anticipatory capacity and knowledge transfer, and conceptually validated against real-world wildfire scenarios and stakeholder needs.

### Task 8.5: Applicability and usability in a coordination cell

Live, scenario-based testing at governance level (a coordination cell) evaluates strategic and societal aspects, aligned with Task 5.5 at command post level.
