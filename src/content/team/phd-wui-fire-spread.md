## Role in FireRisk

Doctoral position based at Hasselt University (Centre for Environmental Sciences), co-supervised by PXL. Works on the regional mapping of the wildland–urban interface ([WP1](/research/wp1-wui-mapping)) and on developing a high-resolution fire spread model for Flanders ([WP4](/research/wp4-fire-spread-modelling)).

## Biography

Jarne Lecoutre is a PhD researcher at the Centre for Environmental Sciences (CMK) at Hasselt University. Within the project, he focuses on the regional mapping of the wildland–urban interface (WUI) and developing a high-resolution fire spread model tailored to the Flemish landscape.

Jarne holds an MSc in Bioscience Engineering (Land, Water, and Climate) from Ghent University and a Postgraduate degree in Applied Artificial Intelligence from Erasmushogeschool Brussel. Prior to starting his doctoral research, he spent over three years working as a GIS engineer and data engineer, building automated spatial data pipelines, WebGIS applications, and cloud-based analytics for environmental and public sector projects. His research combines geospatial modeling, remote sensing, and machine learning to improve wildfire risk assessment and mitigation strategies in densely populated regions.

## Research interests

- Wildfire risk assessment and high-resolution fire spread modeling

- Wildland–Urban Interface (WUI) mapping and spatial vulnerability

- Geospatial engineering, remote sensing, and spatial data pipelines

- Applied machine learning and AI for environmental and climate modeling
